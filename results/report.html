Summary

This report covers 19 samples (9 Early, 10 Late) sequenced on the 16S rRNA V4 region (Illumina MiSeq, 2×250 bp), plus one mock community used as an accuracy control.

  • 152,360 raw read pairs were processed into 232 amplicon sequence variants (ASVs); a median of 81.5% of reads per sample passed every quality step.
  • Accuracy check: in the mock community, 20 of 20 ASVs matched a known reference strain exactly, accounting for 100.0% of mock reads.
  • Community composition differs between groups (PERMANOVA R² = 0.43, p < 0.001), explaining about 43% of between-sample variation.
  • Within-sample diversity does not differ significantly between groups (observed richness p = 0.094; Shannon index p = 0.111), so the groups differ in community makeup rather than in richness.
  • 15 of 46 genera differ significantly in abundance after correcting for multiple testing.

1. Read quality and processing

Quality profiles were used to set truncation lengths of 240 bp (forward) and 160 bp (reverse), where median quality stays well above Q30.

Reads were filtered (max 2 expected errors, no ambiguous bases), denoised with DADA2, merged, length-filtered to 250 to 256 bp, and screened for chimeras. Before ecological analysis, 4 off-target ASVs (mitochondrial, chloroplast, or unassigned at kingdom level; 0.13% of reads) were removed.

2. Accuracy check: mock community

The mock community is a defined mix of known bacterial strains, represented by 32 reference 16S sequences. The pipeline recovered 20 ASVs from it, 20 of which match a reference sequence exactly (100.0% of mock reads). No spurious ASVs were produced, so the error-correction step is not inflating diversity.

4. Between-sample (beta) diversity

PERMANOVA on Bray-Curtis distances shows group explains 43% of community variation (p < 0.001, 9,999 permutations). Group dispersions also differ, so part of the PERMANOVA signal may reflect differences in within-group spread; interpret with that in mind.

Methods

Paired-end reads were processed in R with DADA2 (v1.30.0). Reads were truncated at 240/160 bp and filtered at maxEE = 2; error rates were learned per run and used for sample inference. Pairs were merged with a minimum 12 bp overlap, sequences outside 250 to 256 bp were removed, and chimeras were removed with the consensus method. Mitochondrial, chloroplast, and kingdom-unassigned ASVs were removed before community analysis. Taxonomy was assigned with the naive Bayesian classifier against SILVA v138.2 (minimum bootstrap 50) with exact-match species assignment. Community analyses used phyloseq (v1.46.0) and vegan (v2.6.4). Alpha diversity was computed on data rarefied to 2,516 reads per sample. Beta diversity used Bray-Curtis distances on relative abundances, tested with PERMANOVA (adonis2) and homogeneity of dispersions (betadisper), each with 9,999 permutations.
